隱私權政策

更新日期 2026 年 11 月 26 日。本政策適用於 NickHuang Studio 發行的《銀河之心 Galaxy Heart》iOS 與 Android 版本。

遊戲資料與雲端存檔

你可以直接遊玩。遊戲會在裝置上儲存通關進度、最高分、星等、設定、能量、道具和購買權益快取，讓你下次開啟時繼續遊玩。如果使用雲端存檔，iOS 會使用 Game Center 驗證平台身分，並將通關進度、最高分和星等存到 iCloud 的 CloudKit 私有資料庫。Android 使用 Google Play 遊戲服務的 Saved Games 同步這些遊戲紀錄。現行版使用平台帳號和存檔服務，遊戲內沒有獨立的 NickHuang Studio 註冊表單。

應用程式內購買

iOS 透過 Apple StoreKit 處理購買，Android 透過 Google Play Billing 處理購買。遊戲使用平台提供的商品及交易資料驗證購買、開通並恢復權益。付款資訊由 Apple 或 Google 處理，遊戲不會取得你的付款卡號。

獎勵廣告與隱私選項

你可以自行選擇觀看 Google AdMob 獎勵廣告，以補充能量或獲得特定道具。為了投放廣告、統計成效及防止詐欺，Google 可能處理 IP 位址及由此推估的大致位置、裝置或應用程式識別資料、廣告互動與診斷資訊。實際處理視平台、裝置設定、所在地及適用的同意選項而異。適用時，可在遊戲的「系統設定」開啟「廣告隱私選項」。

保存、刪除與資料請求

本機紀錄會保留到資料被清除。Android 可在系統設定清除應用程式資料，iOS 可刪除 App 清除裝置上的副本。雲端存檔和商店交易紀錄由對應的平台管理，清除本機副本後，雲端備份或購買紀錄仍可能存在。

要查詢、更正或刪除我們處理的資料（包含舊測試版可能建立的紀錄），請寄信至 warhammerlieutenant@gmail.com，註明遊戲名稱、平台和希望處理的資料。我們可能需要確認身分，並會告知處理方式及依法需要保留的資料。請勿寄送密碼或付款卡號。

Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026. This policy applies to the iOS and Android editions of Galaxy Heart published by NickHuang Studio.

Game data and cloud saves

You can play without signing in through the game. The app stores level progress, high scores, stars, settings, energy, items, and cached purchase entitlements on your device so you can resume play. If you use cloud saves, iOS uses Game Center for platform identity and iCloud CloudKit private storage for progress, high scores, and stars. Android syncs the same game records through Google Play Games Services Saved Games. The current game uses platform accounts and has no separate NickHuang Studio sign-up form.

In-app purchases

Apple StoreKit handles purchases on iOS and Google Play Billing handles purchases on Android. The game uses product and transaction data from the store to verify purchases and grant or restore entitlements. Apple or Google handles payment details. The game does not receive your payment card number.

Rewarded ads and privacy options

You can choose to watch Google AdMob rewarded ads to refill energy or receive certain items. Google may process IP addresses and approximate locations inferred from them, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Processing depends on your platform, settings, region, and applicable consent choices. Where available, use Ad Privacy Options in the game's settings.

Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy

Retention, deletion, and data requests

Local records stay on the device until cleared. On Android, clear app data in system settings. On iOS, delete the app to remove the local copy. Platform-managed cloud backups and purchase records may remain after local deletion.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of data we handle, including records from earlier test versions, contact warhammerlieutenant@gmail.com with the game name, platform, and the data you want us to address. We may need to verify your identity and will explain how we handle the request and any retention required by law. Please leave passwords and card numbers out of your email.
